# Privacy Policy

AWBW Enhancements Plus doesn't collect, sell or send anyone your data.

- **Where it runs:** only on pages of Advance Wars By Web (`https://awbw.amarriner.com/*`).
- **What it stores:** your extension options, move planner snapshots and the AWBW display settings it needs (such as
  your CO portrait style), in the browser's extension storage. If you use Chrome sync, your browser may sync the options
  between your own devices; that's handled by your browser, not by this extension.
- **What it sends:** requests go only to Advance Wars By Web, the same kind the site itself makes (for example, loading a
  game's players when you open the move planner panel). Nothing goes to the developer or to anyone else, and there are
  no analytics or ads.
- **Downloads:** if you turn on automatic replay renaming, it renames replay files you download from AWBW. It doesn't
  read or change any other download.

Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/carsonhu/awbw_enhancements/issues>.
